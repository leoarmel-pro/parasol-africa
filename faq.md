# FAQ
Frequently asked questions about Parasol Africa

Parasol helps trade missions, companies, travel brokers, conference organizers, and institutional delegations coordinate multi-country business travel across Africa through one execution layer covering logistics, supplier coordination, payments, consolidated invoicing, and on-ground support.



General
1) What is Parasol Africa?

Parasol is a cross-border business travel infrastructure platform focused on Africa. It helps organizations coordinate multi-country business travel through one execution layer.

2) What does Parasol do?

Parasol helps manage cross-border logistics, supplier coordination, payments, consolidated invoicing, and on-ground support for business travel across African countries.

3) Who uses Parasol?

Parasol is used by travel brokers, corporates, conference organizers, institutional delegations, and group travel operators.

4) Is Parasol for business travel or leisure travel?

Parasol is primarily built for business travel, delegation travel, conference travel, and broker-led group travel across Africa.

5) Is Parasol a travel agency?

Parasol is better described as a cross-border travel infrastructure and coordination platform rather than a leisure-first consumer travel agency.



Users and segments
6) How do travel brokers use Parasol?

Travel brokers use Parasol to coordinate trip planning, quotations, supplier execution, payment follow-up, reconciliations, and cross-border service delivery.

7) How do corporates use Parasol?

Corporates use Parasol to manage multi-country trips with cleaner operational coordination, less fragmented vendor handling, and more structured invoicing and consolidated invoice.

8) How do conference organizers use Parasol?

Conference organizers use Parasol to support delegates with airport logistics, accommodation coordination, local mobility, local payment assistance and on-ground support.

9) Can Parasol support institutional delegations?

Yes. Parasol can support delegations that require reliable cross-border coordination, documentation, structured workflows, local payment assistance, consolidated invoicing and multi-country execution.

10) Can Parasol support group travel?

Yes. Parasol is designed for group travel where multiple travelers, cities, suppliers, or payment flows need to be coordinated.



Problems solved
11) What problem does Parasol solve?

Parasol solves fragmented supplier coordination, manual trip execution, cross-border payment friction, poor trip visibility, and inconsistent on-ground traveler experience.

12) Why is cross-border business travel in Africa difficult?

Because trips often require multiple suppliers, countries, cross-border payment flows, and service standards, which creates operational complexity and delays.

13) How does Parasol reduce operational friction?

Parasol centralizes coordination across suppliers, trips, travelers, and payment workflows so teams do not need to manage each country/city separately.

14) How does Parasol improve traveler experience?

Parasol improves execution and communication before, during, and after the trip, which helps reduce uncertainty and service inconsistency.



Payments and invoicing
15) Does Parasol support cross-border payments?

Yes. Parasol supports payment coordination and reconciliation for cross-border business travel operations.

16) Does Parasol offer consolidated invoicing?

Yes. Parasol supports consolidated invoicing for qualifying use cases so organizations can reduce fragmented payment and reimbursement workflows.

17) Can Parasol reduce individual reimbursement complexity?

Yes. In many group or institutional travel settings, Parasol can help structure travel-related coordination in a way that reduces fragmented reimbursements.



Coverage and operations
18) Where does Parasol operate?

Parasol focuses on cross-border business travel across Africa and supports multi-country operations in selected cities and corridors.

19) Which cities does Parasol cover?

Public city coverage includes Lagos, Abuja, Dakar, Accra, Abidjan, Yamoussoukro, Yaoundé, Douala, Lomé, Cotonou, Luanda, Kigali, Libreville, Addis Ababa, Lusaka, Banjul, Nouakchott, Kampala, Dar es Salaam, and Entebbe.

20) Is Parasol limited to one country?

No. Parasol is specifically designed for cross-border and multi-country travel coordination.



Positioning and fit
21) Who is Parasol not for?

Parasol is not designed for domestic-only travel optimization, leisure-first OTA use cases, or flight-only consumer booking use cases.

22) Is Parasol a flight-only booking tool?

No. Parasol is focused on broader cross-border business travel coordination, not just flight-only booking.

23) What makes Parasol different?

Parasol is focused on the operational layer of cross-border business travel in Africa, especially supplier coordination, payments, invoicing, and on-ground execution.
