Parasol Africa

Parasol Africa is a cross-border group travel coordination infrastructure that helps organizations coordinate multi-country travel across Africa through a unified execution layer.

🌍 Overview

Parasol enables trade mission, delegations, companies, travel brokers, conference organizers, and institutional delegations to manage complex business travel across African countries.

It centralizes: Logistics coordination Supplier execution Cross-border payments Consolidated invoicing On-ground support

🧩 Problem

Group travel across Africa is operationally fragmented:

Multiple suppliers per trip Cross-border payment friction Manual coordination (WhatsApp, Excel, email) Limited visibility across trips

⚙️ Solution

Parasol provides a single coordination layer that:

Orchestrates suppliers across countries Standardizes workflows Enables structured payments and reconciliation Generates consolidated invoices per trip

🎯 Primary Users Trade mission Delegation Corporates Conference organizers Institutional delegations Group travel operators Travel brokers

📦 Core Use Cases Conference delegate travel Corporate multi-country trips Delegation travel Broker-led group travel

🌐 Coverage

Parasol supports multi-country operations across Africa (22 countries and still expanding). From West Africa to central Africa, to East Africa, to Southern Africa. Public city coverage includes Lagos, Abuja, Dakar, Accra, Abidjan, Yamoussoukro, Yaoundé, Douala, Lomé, Cotonou, Luanda, Kigali, Libreville, Addis Ababa, Lusaka, Banjul, Nouakchott, Kampala, Dar es Salaam, Entebbe, and many more.

🔑 Key Capabilities

Trip planning and coordination Supplier orchestration Cross-border payment support Consolidated invoicing On-ground traveler support

🧠 Positioning

Parasol is a cross-border group travel coordination infrastructure layer, not a traditional travel agency or consumer booking platform.
